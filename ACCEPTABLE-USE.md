# Acceptable Use Policy

discord-mcp is built for authorized, accountable Discord operations. Do not
use the project to:

- raid, spam, harass, or intentionally disrupt a Discord server or community;
- access or change a bot, account, server, channel, or other resource without
  the authorization of its owner or responsible administrator;
- steal, expose, solicit, or misuse credentials, private content, or personal
  data;
- evade Discord controls or discord-mcp confirmation, permission, allowlist,
  audit, rate-limit, or other safety controls; or
- violate applicable law or Discord's applicable terms and policies.

Violations may result in refusal of project support, removal from project
community spaces, or reporting to the affected service or appropriate
authority. This policy governs participation in and support from the Cappyeo
project community. It does not add to or subtract from the copyright and patent
permissions in the [software license](LICENSE).
