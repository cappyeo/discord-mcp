# Contributing

Contributions that improve safe, reliable, caller-owned Discord operations are
welcome. Before opening a pull request:

1. Keep the change focused and explain the behavior it changes.
2. Add or update tests for externally observable behavior.
3. Run the smallest relevant test command, then the affected package's
   typecheck.
4. Disclose third-party code, assets, generated material, and their licenses.
5. Read the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md).

Every human-authored pull request must include this exact statement in its
description:

> I have read and agree to the Cappyeo Contributor License Agreement 1.0.

You retain ownership of your contribution. The agreement gives Cappyeo the
rights needed to publish it under the project's public license and, where
appropriate, separate commercial terms. If your employer or another legal
entity owns the contribution, contact
[404jhm@gmail.com](mailto:404jhm@gmail.com) before submitting it.

Automated dependency-update pull requests may be accepted without a human CLA
when they contain no independently authored contribution. Maintainers will not
merge a human-authored contribution without a recorded CLA acceptance.
